Toofan

Toofan was a hero I was responsible for designing in Heart Electric.

Toofan was designed to fulfil a player fantasy that none of the other Heart Electric heroes fit in so far - that of the "tank".
In an online multiplayer FPS, the role of a tank isn't obvious compared to e.g. an RPG, as anything that would passively turn him into too much of a bullet sponge or force opponents to attack him by taking hard control would feel bad for the opponents and interrupt our principles of gunplay first.
Instead, Toofan was designed to be a character that could run in front of his teammates towards enemies, with situational abilities that would be obviously telegraphed to the enemy to protect his team.

Toofan was designed to be a large presence with a larger frame and hitbox to compensate for a larger health pool.
With the relatively low ttk of the game, this wouldn't be enough to make Toofan a bullet sponge. In previous playtests, we had observed that the character with a larger frame would be more likely to be a target for opponents. In Toofan's case, this would be an advantage as long as he had tools to play around it.

Abilities

Toofan's abilities were designed to assist in fulfilling the fantasy around a Hero who runs in front to take the focus and soak up hits for the rest of his team.
He needed to be disruptive and have abilities that commanded attention but without any "hard" crowd control.
The theme of someone who spins to create tornadoes was already present and decided upon from the concepting stages, so this was adapted into a defender playstyle.
