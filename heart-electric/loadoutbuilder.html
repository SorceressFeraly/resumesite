Loadout Builder

The “Loadout” feature in Heart Electric is an umbrella feature that allows players to bring a predetermined selection of Guns, Gun Mods, Optics & Perks into a match of HE, similar to the “create-a-class” feature in the Call of Duty.

The purpose of the Loadout system is to allow the player to tinker with the gameplay in a sandbox manner, encourage the players to find creative ways of finding new plays, create social discourse & new content types to increase the creativity space. It should motivate players engaged by theorycrafting and optimization as well as provide venues for content creators. By tweaking the Loadout system in our live patches, we will be able to provide a revolving meta for the live service.

The Loadout should motivate players through gameplay unlocks vertically in the short term (perks, and guns, ~30 days to fully unlock) and horizontally in the long term (mods and mod slots, ~300 days to fully unlock.)

Summary

In HE, players are able to select a Loadout they want to use in a match during the pre-match flow in combination with selecting a character.

  • Loadouts are created outside of the core match loop. Players are able to create Custom Loadouts or select one of the Preset Loadouts.
  • Every Loadout contains two primary weapons with mods, as well as a set of Perks (1 Neural Core + 2 Subroutines).
    • Every primary weapon can be customised by using the gun customization system, which allows the player to choose up to four (4) Gun Mods and a Gun Optic.
    • Selecting the gun-related cosmetics (Gun Paints, Gun Skins & Charms) will also be selectable within the Custom Loadout system.
  • There are several “unlocks” connected to the Loadout system that will impact both the FTUX and long-term progression:
    • Additional Preset Loadouts will be unlocked in early Account Levels.
    • The Custom Loadouts feature and ability to create your own loadouts will be unlocked after a few hours of playtime.
    • Every gun has its own level, which allows players to unlock additional Mod Slots & Gun Mods, as well as additional Gun Optics.
    • Additional Perks will be unlocked with Account Level.
