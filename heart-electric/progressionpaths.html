Progression Systems

The progression systems in Heart Electric were designed to encompasses the reward paths of power, content, and cosmetics, as well as how the players would be able to earn them.

Summary and Goals

Our progression goal is to retain the tourists (defined as people checking out the game due to novelty rather than core engagement) for the first 30 days by offering new gameplay features and power progression, and unlock progression systems with more long-term depth to give the superfans goals they feel are worth playing the game they love over.

How we do this:

  1. Provide engagement for the first 50h/28 days of gameplay by providing ways to unlock content that can be used in the match loop.
  2. Provide ways to let the players pursue their own agency in figuring out how to optimise their progression and get the most value out of their playtime.
  3. Provide longer term 500h+ unlocks that will continue to provide for our superfans.

By the end of 28 days, the people who joined because of HE being flavour of the month are likely to churn regardless, so up to the first month is where we want the power progression motivations to be the strongest.

  • People who are starting to churn will have unlocked most guns and some basic characters, they can likely be reactivated by new game modes or if the game has exciting content updates.
  • People who like the game and want more reason to explore its mechanics deeper have only begun to gain gun mod progression by unlocking a few mods and are opening up more slots for mods on their most commonly used weapons.

This allows them to start diving deeper into the systems and experiment, and since they know what rewards to look for, they don’t mind the longer progression without rewards at this point.
